# PERSONAS.md: discovery inputs

Source: the Customer Complaint Management discovery document (five personas and six themes). Use these to decide what to build and to write the persona-to-feature summary.

## Personas

| # | Persona | How complaints arrive | Main frustration | What would help |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Support rep, online retail | WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, phone | Can't tell if a colleague already replied or promised a refund | One record with order reference, owner, status, last action |
| 2 | Support rep, internet provider | Phone, WhatsApp, walk-ins | Customers call repeatedly because updates sit in a separate group chat | Clear status, assigned owner, history of updates |
| 3 | Support rep, education services | Phone, email, WhatsApp | Complaints pass between teams with no clear owner | Priority, assignment, notes on what was already tried |
| 4 | Business manager, logistics | Team updates in meetings, spreadsheets | No current view of what is open, urgent and who owns it | Dashboard and a list of what needs attention |
| 5 | Business owner, professional services | Goes to whichever staff member the customer knows | Can't tell if an issue is being handled or just waiting | A simple system that needs little training |

## Themes and how the product answers them

| Theme | Feature that answers it |
| --- | --- |
| Fragmented complaint records | One central complaint list with unique references |
| Unclear ownership | "Assigned to" field plus assignment events |
| Limited status visibility | Status pills, filters, 4-card stat strip |
| Repeated customer follow-ups | Clear status and a full timeline of updates |
| Need for urgency handling | Priority levels and the High priority card |
| Need for context during handover | Internal notes on the same timeline |

## Persona-to-feature summary (fill in during Phase 1)
For each persona, write one line: *Persona → biggest pain → feature in the PRD that answers it.* If a feature in the PRD matches no persona, flag it for removal.
